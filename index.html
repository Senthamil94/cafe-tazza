Our story

A cup, a griddle,
and a lot of regulars

“Tazza” means cup — and that’s where this started. Chai for the neighbours, then a dosa griddle, then a bakery case that had to be egg-free because half of Dublin asked for it. Today families from Dublin, Pleasanton, San Ramon and Livermore come for breakfast idli, an after-school plate of chaat, or a cake with someone’s name piped on it.
